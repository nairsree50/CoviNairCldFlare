```

```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.

## Article Image Generation Guidelines

When generating images or crafting image prompts for articles:
- **No Decorative Filler:** Never generate generic lifestyle stock illustrations (e.g., people smiling at tablets, holding house keys, standing in showrooms, or sitting at generic desks).
- **Value-Add Mandatory Rule:** Every image MUST convey either:
  1. An **overall conceptual summary / visual thesis** of the article, OR
  2. A **clear explanatory diagram of the critical mathematics, formulas, ratios, or cost waterfalls** discussed in the article.
- **Approved Design Frameworks:**
  * *The Cost Iceberg:* Advertised price above water vs. hidden statutory/builder/fit-out costs submerged.
  * *The Cash Flow Offset Schematic:* Inflows vs. recurring liabilities = net effective outlay.
  * *The Compounding Divergence Curve:* Starting early vs. starting late, or fee drag over 20-30 years.
  * *The Ratio / Affordability Gauge:* 28/36 debt limits, 50/30/20 allocation wheels, safe withdrawal percentages.
  * *The Multi-Stage Timeline / Waterfall:* Clear cost allocations by phase or milestone.
- **Visual Style:**
  * Modern Swiss-style financial infographic / vector editorial schematic.
  * 16:9 widescreen format (1376×768 or 1920×1080).
  * Warm editorial palette: Cream background, terracotta warning/highlight badges, navy blue structure, muted sage accents.
  * Data-rich, clear section labels, and concrete Indian Rupee (₹) / percentage metrics matching the article text.
